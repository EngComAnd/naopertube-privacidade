2. What Não Pertube does with your data

  • We ask for no sign-up, have no server and do not receive your data. Call screening happens on your device and does not use the internet.
  • Callers' numbers: the app keeps, only on the device, the number, the day and time of each call, whether it was allowed or rejected and why. By default this stays 3 days; you choose 0 to 365 days in Settings (with 0, nothing is kept) and everything is deleted automatically afterwards.
  • Your decisions: the numbers you add, allow or block, nicknames, groups (name, days and hours) and which group each number belongs to stay only on the device until you delete them. Nicknames and group names are free text and may reveal your relationship with the person: choose carefully.
  • Contacts: if you allow it, the app only looks up your contacts at the moment of the call, to let people you know get through. Nothing is copied.
  • Repeat callers: the "Let repeat callers through" option counts, only in the app's memory and for a few minutes, repeated calls from the same number, so a possible emergency can ring. None of this is stored.
  • Notifications: the rejected-call alert never shows a number, name, nickname or group. Android itself may show a "missed call" notification with the number; the app does not control that.
  • Exporting and importing the list: the file is only created when you ask, where you choose, and the app never sends it. It is not protected: keep it only where you trust.
  • The app does not access the system call log, text messages or the microphone, and does not record audio.
  • Google backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for the app: the list, groups and history do not go to the cloud and do not come back after a reinstall. Export the list before uninstalling.
  • You delete the history, the list or the groups in Settings, or everything by uninstalling the app.

3. Ads (Google AdMob) and support

  • The app is free and shows a single banner, only on the Settings screen. There is no ad at launch or on the screens you use.
  • The ad is provided by Google (AdMob), which may process device data such as the advertising ID, the IP address and technical information to deliver and measure the ad and prevent fraud. Google acts as controller of that data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy. The app gives Google no number, name, group, history or contact data.
  • We always request non-personalized ads, in every region.
  • Where the law requires (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, for example), Google shows a consent form before the first ad. Without consent there is no ad, and the app keeps working fully.
  • You can review or withdraw your choice in Settings → Ad privacy, when your region requires it.
  • Voluntary support and the "Remove ads" purchase are handled and recorded by Google Play. The app does not receive or store payment data.

5. Sharing and international transfer

The app's data (numbers, names, nicknames, groups, schedules, history and contacts) never leaves the device and is not shared. The only sharing is with Google, through the ads SDK and Google Play, as described above. Google may process that data in other countries; the safeguards it uses are described in its policy (data transfers). Não Pertube makes no transfer on its own, because it does not receive your data.

6. Children and teenagers

Não Pertube is not aimed at children: it is intended for people aged 13 or over.

7. Your rights (LGPD, arts. 18 and 19)

You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information about sharing and withdrawal of consent, at no cost. Since the app keeps no data about you on its own server, almost everything is solved on the device: Settings → Delete history, Delete list, Delete groups, or withdraw permissions in Android settings. For any request: engcomandre@gmail.com. You may also petition the ANPD.

8. Security

The database stays in the app's private storage, outside automatic backup. Screenshots are blocked on the app's screens, and the system's technical logs carry no numbers, names, nicknames or group names.
